Why Tiny Tock

Made for the little gaps in your day.

Waiting for the kettle, between classes, on the bus, or when your brain needs five minutes off. Tiny Tock games load in a blink and are easy to pick up. Most are designed so one round fits a short break.

  1. 01

  2. 02

  3. 03

Questions

Good to know

Are the games really free?

Yes. Every game on Tiny Tock is free to play. The site is supported by ads, which we keep out of the way of the games themselves.

Do I need to download anything or make an account?

No. Games run in your web browser and start instantly. There is nothing to install and no sign-up.

Where are my high scores saved?

Your best scores are stored in your browser on your own device, so they are private to you. Clearing your browser data will reset them.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Every game works with touch controls such as swipes and on-screen buttons, and with a keyboard on a computer.
