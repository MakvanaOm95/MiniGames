Privacy policy

Your privacy, in plain words.

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit Tiny Tock (tinytock.com), how it is used, and the choices you have. We’ve tried to keep it short and clear.

The short version

  • You don’t need an account, and we never ask for your name or email to play.
  • Your high scores and settings are stored only in your own browser.
  • We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads and measure them.
  • We use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits so we know which games people enjoy.

Information stored on your device

Tiny Tock uses your browser’s local storage to remember things like your best score in each game, whether sound is muted, and whether you prefer light or dark mode. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device. Tiny Tock itself does not need cookies for the games to work. However, third parties we work with, such as Google, may set cookies when you visit our site, as described below.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which keeps Tiny Tock free. In connection with this:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are visiting from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before personalised ads or advertising cookies are used, through a consent message provided by Google.

Analytics

We use analytics to understand how many people visit Tiny Tock and which pages and games are popular. This helps us decide which games to make next. We look at totals and trends, such as page views, country, and device type, and not at individuals. We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, and/or Google Analytics, which may use cookies. If we use Google Analytics, you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Hosting and server logs

Tiny Tock is hosted on Cloudflare. Like most websites, Cloudflare may automatically process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages securely and protect the site from abuse.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we’ll use your email address and message only to reply to you. We won’t add you to any mailing list or share your email with anyone.

Children’s privacy

Tiny Tock is designed to be suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and our games do not ask for any personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

Our pages may contain links or ads that lead to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, so please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at hello@tinytock.com or visit our contact page.
