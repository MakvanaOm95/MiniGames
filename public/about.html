About us

Small games, made with care.

Tiny Tock is a home for quick, original games that fit into the little gaps in your day.

Why we made Tiny Tock

Most of us have a few spare minutes here and there: waiting for a download, on a bus, between classes, or when you just need a breather. We wanted a place where you can open a game instantly and finish a satisfying round in that time. No app to install, no account, no endless menus.

So every game on Tiny Tock is tagged with how long a round usually takes. Pick 1, 3 or 5+ minutes on the homepage clock and we’ll show you games that fit.

How we make our games

  • Every game is original. We design and program every game ourselves. We don’t copy or embed games from other websites.
  • Built to feel good. Clear controls, smooth animation, and small sounds that make each move satisfying.
  • Works everywhere. Every game is tested with a keyboard and with touch screens, on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Friendly for everyone. No violence, nothing inappropriate. Suitable for players of all ages, including at school.
  • Respectful of your time. No sign-ups, no pop-ups asking for your email, and your best scores stay on your own device.

How Tiny Tock stays free

Tiny Tock is free to play and is supported by advertising. We place ads around the games, never on top of them, so they don’t get in the way while you play. You can read more in our privacy policy.
