2048

Puzzle About 5+ min

Slide with ← ↑ → ↓ or WASD. Press P to pause. Swipe on the board to slide the tiles.

About 2048

2048 is a simple sliding puzzle that’s very hard to put down. Every move slides all the tiles on the board in one direction. When two tiles with the same number bump into each other, they merge into one tile worth their sum: 2 + 2 makes 4, 4 + 4 makes 8, and so on up to 2048.

Each move also adds a new 2 (or sometimes a 4) in an empty square, so the board slowly fills up. Our version has smooth sliding, a satisfying pop on every merge, and toy colors that change as your numbers grow. Reaching 2048 isn’t the end: keep going for 4096 and beyond.

How to play

  1. Press Play. Two tiles appear on the board.
  2. Slide all tiles up, down, left or right with the arrow keys, or swipe on a phone.
  3. Matching tiles that touch merge into one. Your score goes up by the new tile’s value.
  4. The round ends when the board is full and no tiles can merge.
Controls
SlideArrow keys, WASD, or swipe
PauseP or Esc, or the pause button
RestartSpace or Enter on the game-over screen

Tips to score higher

  • Pick a corner and stay there. Keep your biggest tile in one corner (for example bottom-left) and build toward it.
  • Use only two or three directions. If your big tile lives bottom-left, mostly press down and left. Use right when you must, and avoid up.
  • Keep a row full. A full bottom row can’t shift sideways by accident, so your big tiles stay put.
  • Line numbers up in order. A row like 64-32-16-8 can collapse into one big tile in a few quick moves.
  • Think before you slide up. One move in the wrong direction can drop a small tile into your corner and block it.
