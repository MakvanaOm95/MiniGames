Snake

Arcade About 3 min

Steer with ← ↑ → ↓ or WASD. Press P to pause. Swipe on the board or use the buttons to steer.

About Snake

Snake is one of the best-loved arcade games ever made. You guide a hungry snake around a board and eat apples. Every apple makes the snake a little longer and a little faster, and the board fills up quickly.

Our version keeps the simple classic rules and adds smooth movement, satisfying sounds, and a golden clock that appears after every five apples. Grab it before its timer runs out for 3 bonus points.

How to play

  1. Press Play. The snake starts moving to the right on its own.
  2. Steer with the arrow keys or WASD. On a phone, swipe on the board or tap the arrow buttons.
  3. Eat the red apples. Each one is worth 1 point and makes you longer.
  4. The round ends if you run into a wall or into your own body.
Controls
MoveArrow keys, WASD, swipe, or on-screen arrows
PauseP or Esc, or the pause button
RestartSpace or Enter on the game-over screen

Tips to score higher

  • Hug the edges early. Travelling along the walls keeps the middle of the board open for when you’re long.
  • Turn one step early. The snake speeds up with every apple, so plan each turn a moment before you need it.
  • Leave yourself an exit. Before diving toward an apple, check that you won’t be trapped inside a loop of your own body.
  • Only chase the golden clock if it’s close. 3 points is nice, but a risky dash across the board can end your run.
  • Queue your turns. You can press two directions quickly (like up then left) to make a tight U-shape.
