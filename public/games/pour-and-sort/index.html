Pour & Sort

Puzzle About 3 min

Click a tube, then another to pour. Or press the tube’s number key. Z undoes. Press P to pause. Tap a tube, then tap another tube to pour into it.

About Pour & Sort

Pour & Sort is a calm, colorful water sort puzzle with a little twist: a 3-minute clock. Each tube holds up to four layers of paint, all mixed up. Pour from tube to tube until every tube holds just one color, then a new puzzle appears with one more color to sort.

You can only pour onto the same color or into an empty tube, so the two empty tubes are your best friends. Every puzzle is checked by our solver before you see it, so there’s always a way to finish. Made a mess? Undo as many times as you like. It only costs you time.

How to play

  1. Press Play. The 3-minute clock starts.
  2. Tap a tube to lift it, then tap another tube to pour its top color in.
  3. You can pour onto the same color or into an empty tube, as long as there’s room.
  4. Fill every tube with a single color to solve the puzzle. Solve as many as you can before time runs out!
Controls
Pick up / pourTap or click a tube, or press its number key (1–9)
UndoThe Undo button, or Z
New puzzleThe New puzzle button (same number of colors)
PauseP or Esc, or the pause button

Tips to score higher

  • Keep an empty tube free. Empty tubes let you move any color. Fill one only when it helps finish a color.
  • Finish one color at a time. Pick the color that’s closest to the top of the tubes and gather it first.
  • Look before you pour. A layer buried at the bottom takes the most work. Plan how to dig it out.
  • Stuck? Undo or skip. Undo is free, and New puzzle gives you a fresh one with the same number of colors.
