About Tock Tones

Tock Tones is a memory game for your eyes and ears. The toy has four colored pads, and each one plays its own musical note. Each round it plays a pattern by lighting up the pads one after another. Your job is to repeat the pattern in exactly the same order.

Every round adds one more step to the end of the pattern, and the toy plays a little faster as you go. One wrong pad ends the game. Because every pad has its own note, you can remember the pattern by its tune as well as its colors.

How to play

  1. Press Play and watch the pad that lights up.
  2. When the center says Your turn, press the same pad.
  3. Next round, the toy plays the same pattern plus one new step. Repeat the whole thing.
  4. Keep going as long as you can. One mistake ends the game.
Controls
Press a padTap or click it, or ↑ top, → right, ↓ bottom, ← left
PauseP or Esc, or the pause button

Tips to score higher

  • Listen, don’t just look. The four notes make a little tune. Humming it back is often easier than remembering colors.
  • Name the moves. Saying “up, right, right, left” in your head turns the pattern into words.
  • Break it into chunks. Remember long patterns in groups of three or four, like a phone number.
  • Only the end is new. Each round repeats the last pattern and adds one step, so focus on the new one.
  • Don’t rush. There’s no time limit on your turn. Take a breath before you start.
