Tower Tock

Casual About 1 min

Click the board or press Space to drop the block. Press P to pause. Tap anywhere on the board to drop the block.

About Tower Tock

Tower Tock is a one-tap stacking game. A painted toy block slides back and forth above your tower, and you tap to drop it. If it lands a little off to one side, the part hanging over the edge is sliced off and tumbles away, so your next block is narrower. Miss the tower completely and it’s game over.

Land a block exactly on top and you get a Perfect!, which keeps the block at full width. Get three perfects in a row and the block even grows back a little. The blocks slide faster as the tower climbs, so every floor is a tiny test of timing. It’s a perfect one-minute break.

How to play

  1. Press Play. A block starts sliding above the tower.
  2. Tap when it’s lined up with the block below. Any overhang gets sliced off.
  3. Line it up exactly for a Perfect! Three in a row makes your block wider again.
  4. Keep stacking. The game ends when a block misses the tower completely.
Controls
Drop blockTap or click the board, or press Space
PauseP or Esc, or the pause button

Tips to score higher

  • Watch the edge, not the middle. Keep your eye on one edge of the tower and tap when the sliding block’s edge lines up with it.
  • Find the rhythm. Each block moves at a steady speed, so after a bounce or two you can feel the right moment coming.
  • Chase perfects early. The blocks are slow at the start. Stacking perfects then keeps your blocks wide for the fast floors later.
  • Don’t rush. The block keeps sliding until you tap, so you can always wait for the next pass.
