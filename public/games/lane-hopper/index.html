Lane Hopper

Endless Runner About 1 min

Use ← → (or A D) to hop lanes. Press P to pause. Tap the left or right side of the road to hop that way.

About Lane Hopper

Lane Hopper is a quick dodging game on a toy play-mat road. Your little red car drives forward all by itself, and the road has three lanes. Traffic cones, alphabet blocks and parked toy trucks come at you, and you hop left or right to slip past them.

Every row of obstacles leaves at least one lane open, so there’s always a way through if you’re quick. Grab the gold stars for bonus points. The road gets faster the longer you drive, and one bump ends the run. It’s a perfect game for a one-minute break.

How to play

  1. Press Play. Your car starts driving in the middle lane.
  2. Tap the left or right side of the road (or press ← →) to hop one lane that way.
  3. Find the open lane in each row and get into it before the obstacles reach you.
  4. Collect stars for +10 points each. One bump and it’s game over!
Controls
Hop leftTap the left side, or press ← / A
Hop rightTap the right side, or press → / D
PauseP or Esc, or the pause button

Tips to score higher

  • Look ahead. Watch the row after the next one, so you’re already moving toward the open lane.
  • The middle lane is flexible. From the middle you’re only one hop from any lane.
  • Walls come in pairs later. As you speed up, more rows block two lanes. Hop early rather than late.
  • Skip risky stars. A star is only 10 points. A crash ends everything.
