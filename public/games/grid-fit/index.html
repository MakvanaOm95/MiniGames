Grid Fit

Puzzle About 5+ min

Drag a piece onto the grid, or press 1 2 3 to pick one, move it with the arrow keys and press Enter. Press P to pause. Drag a piece onto the grid. Or tap a piece, then tap where it should go.

About Grid Fit

Grid Fit is a relaxing block puzzle on an 8 by 8 grid. You get three colorful pieces at a time: long bars, squares, L-shapes and more. Drag each one onto the grid wherever it fits. When a whole row or column is filled, it clears away and gives you more room.

There’s no timer, so you can take your time and plan. The catch is that pieces can’t be turned, and you have to place all three before you get new ones. When none of your pieces fit anywhere, the game is over. Clear several lines at once for a much bigger bonus!

How to play

  1. Press Play. Three pieces appear under the grid.
  2. Drag a piece onto the grid. A shadow shows where it will land, and lines about to clear light up.
  3. Fill a whole row or column to clear it.
  4. Place all three pieces to get three new ones. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.

Tips to score higher

  • Save room for the big ones. Keep a 3 × 3 space open whenever you can. The big square and long bars need it.
  • Work along the edges. Fill rows and columns from the sides so the middle stays open.
  • Clear lines together. Two lines at once scores 40, three scores 90. Setting up combos pays off.
  • Place the awkward piece first. If one piece only fits in one spot, put it there before the others take it.
