Word Scramble

Word About 1 min

Type the letters, Backspace to undo, Space to shuffle, Enter to skip. Esc pauses. Tap letters to spell the word. Tap a placed letter to take it back.

About Word Scramble

Word Scramble is a quick word puzzle for curious minds. You’re given a jumble of letters, and you have to rearrange them into a real word, such as turning KCOLC into CLOCK. Every word is an everyday one: foods, animals, things around the house and more.

You have 60 seconds to solve as many as you can. Each word scores one point per letter, and the words get longer as you go, so a strong start really pays off. Stuck? Shuffle the letters for a fresh look, or skip to a new word.

How to play

  1. Press Play. The first scrambled word appears and the timer starts.
  2. Tap letters (or type them) to spell the word in the boxes.
  3. When all boxes are full, the word is checked. Right answers score a point per letter.
  4. Use Shuffle to rearrange the letters or Skip to move on. The round ends after 60 seconds.
Controls
Pick a letterTap or type it
UndoTap a placed letter, or Backspace
Shuffle / SkipButtons, or Space / Enter
PauseEsc or the pause button

Tips to score higher

  • Look for common endings. Try -ING, -ER, -LE or -Y at the end. They’re in lots of words.
  • Find the vowels. Placing the A, E, I, O and U often reveals the word’s shape.
  • Try pairs that go together. Letters like CH, SH, TH, CK and QU usually sit side by side.
  • Shuffle when stuck. Seeing the letters in a new order can make the word jump out.
  • Don’t be afraid to skip. A new word costs nothing but a moment. Spending 20 seconds on one word costs a lot more.
