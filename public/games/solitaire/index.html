Solitaire

Classic About 5+ min

Drag cards with your mouse, or click a card to move it automatically. Ctrl+Z undoes. P pauses. Drag cards, or tap a card and it jumps to the best spot.

About Solitaire

Solitaire, also known as Klondike, is the world’s favorite one-player card game. Your goal is to move all 52 cards onto the four foundations at the top right, one pile per suit, in order from Ace up to King.

The seven columns below are where the action happens. You can stack cards there in descending order with alternating colors, such as a red 7 on a black 8. Only a King can move into an empty column. When you’re stuck, tap the stock pile at the top left to turn over a new card. Our version has unlimited undo, lets you tap a card to move it automatically, and finishes the game for you once every card is face up.

How to play

  1. Press Play to deal. Seven columns are laid out, with the top card of each face up.
  2. Move cards between columns, building down in alternating colors (red on black, black on red).
  3. Send Aces to the foundations, then build each suit upward: 2, 3, 4 … all the way to King.
  4. Tap the stock to turn a card when you need more options. Win by moving all 52 cards to the foundations.

Tips to score higher

  • Uncover face-down cards first. Moves that reveal a hidden card in the columns are almost always the best ones.
  • Don’t rush cards to the foundations. Low cards like 2s and 3s are useful for holding other cards in the columns. Send them up when they’re not needed.
  • Save empty columns for Kings. An empty column can only take a King, so clear one when you have a King ready to move.
  • Work on the biggest columns. The columns with the most face-down cards hide the most cards. Try to free them early.
  • Use Undo to explore. If you’re not sure, try a move and undo it. There’s no penalty.
