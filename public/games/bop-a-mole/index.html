Bop-a-Mole

Quick About 1 min

Bop with the number keys: 789 top row, 456 middle, 123 bottom. Or click. Tap the moles as they pop up.

About Bop-a-Mole

Bop-a-Mole is our take on the classic fairground game. Nine holes, a lot of cheeky moles, and just 40 seconds. Each mole pops up for a moment before ducking back down. Tap it before it hides to score a point.

Keep an eye out for golden moles: they’re quick, but worth 3 points. And watch out for the wind-up bombs, which cost you 3 points if you bop them. The moles get faster as the clock runs down, so the last ten seconds are frantic.

How to play

  1. Press Play. The 40-second timer starts.
  2. Tap or click a mole while it’s up to bop it for 1 point.
  3. Gold moles are worth 3 points. Bombs take away 3 points, so leave them alone.
  4. When the timer reaches zero, the round ends.
Controls
BopTap or click a hole, or number keys 1–9
PauseP or Esc, or the pause button
RestartSpace or Enter on the game-over screen

Tips to score higher

  • Look at the whole field. Keep your eyes on the middle hole and use your side vision to spot moles anywhere.
  • Check before you tap. A quick glance for the bomb’s fuse saves you from losing 3 points.
  • Go for gold. Gold moles hide fast, but one is worth three normal ones.
  • Use two thumbs on a phone. Split the board: left thumb for the left column, right thumb for the right.
  • On a keyboard, use the number pad. The 1–9 keys match the holes exactly, like a phone keypad.
