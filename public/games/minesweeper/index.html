Minesweeper

Classic About 5+ min

Click to dig, right-click to flag. Or use ← ↑ → ↓, Enter to dig and F to flag. Tap to dig. Long-press, or switch to Flag mode, to place a flag.

About Minesweeper

Minesweeper is a classic logic puzzle. Ten mines are hidden in a 9×9 field. Dig a square and, if it’s safe, it shows a number: how many mines touch that square, including diagonally. Use those clues to work out where the mines are, flag them, and dig up every safe square.

Your first dig is always safe, and often opens up a big area to get you started. On phones you can long-press to place a flag, or flip the Dig / Flag switch below the field. Your fastest clear is saved as your best time.

How to play

  1. Press Play, then dig any square to start. The first dig is always safe.
  2. Numbers show how many mines touch that square. Empty areas open up automatically.
  3. Flag squares you’re sure hide a mine (right-click, long-press, or Flag mode).
  4. Dig every safe square to win. Digging a mine ends the round.
Controls
DigClick or tap, or Enter on the selected square
FlagRight-click, long-press, Flag mode, or F
Quick digClick a number whose mines are all flagged to dig around it
PauseP or Esc, or the pause button

Tips to score higher

  • Start at the edges of the open area. The numbers along the border of the cleared area give you the most information.
  • A 1 next to one hidden square is a mine. If a 1 touches only one unopened square, that square must be the mine. Flag it.
  • Count flags around numbers. When a number already has that many flags around it, every other square around it is safe.
  • Look for 1-2-1 patterns. Along a straight edge, a 1-2-1 means the mines sit next to the two 1s.
  • Only guess when you must. If logic runs out, guess in a spot with the most open space around it.
