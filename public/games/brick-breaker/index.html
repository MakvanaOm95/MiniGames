Brick Breaker

Arcade About 3 min

Move with ← → or your mouse. Launch with Space. Press P to pause. Drag on the game to move the paddle, and tap to launch.

About Brick Breaker

Brick Breaker is a timeless arcade game. A wall of colorful bricks sits at the top of the screen, and you control a paddle at the bottom. Bounce the ball off your paddle to knock the bricks out one by one, without letting the ball fall past you.

Our version has five level layouts, a ball that speeds up as you progress, and chunky toy-block bricks that burst when you hit them. Bricks near the top are worth more points, and you have three lives to get as far as you can.

How to play

  1. Press Play, then tap or press Space to launch the ball.
  2. Move the paddle with your mouse, finger or the arrow keys to keep the ball in play.
  3. Each brick you break scores points. Higher rows are worth more.
  4. Clear every brick to reach the next level. You lose a life when the ball falls past the paddle.
Controls
MoveMouse, drag with your finger, or ← → / A D
LaunchTap, click, Space or ↑
PauseP or Esc, or the pause button

Tips to score higher

  • Aim with the paddle’s edges. Hitting the ball with the edge of the paddle sends it off at a sharp angle. The middle sends it straight up.
  • Break a hole to the top. Once the ball gets above the bricks, it bounces around up there and clears lots of bricks for free.
  • Stay near the middle. Returning to the center after each hit means you’re never too far from the ball.
  • Watch the ball, not the bricks. Your job is to catch it. The bricks will break on their own.
  • Take your time launching. Move the paddle before you launch to choose where the first shot goes.
