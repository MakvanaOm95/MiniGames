Rock Popper

Arcade About 3 min

← → turn, ↑ thrust, Space fire. Or hold the mouse on the board to aim and fire. Press P to pause. Hold your finger on the board: the rocket turns that way and fires. Hold far from it to fly there.

About Rock Popper

Rock Popper is our toy-box version of a classic arcade space shooter. Your little rocket floats in the middle of space while colorful rocks drift around it. Shoot a big rock and it pops into two medium rocks. Shoot those and they pop into two small ones. The small ones vanish for good.

Clear every rock to start the next wave, with more and faster rocks each time. If a rock bumps into your rocket you lose a life, and after three it’s game over. Everything wraps around the edges, so a rock that drifts off the right side comes back on the left. Watch your back!

How to play

  1. Press Play. Your rocket starts in the middle, blinking. It’s safe for a moment.
  2. Hold your finger or mouse on the board. The rocket turns toward it and fires. Hold far away to fly there.
  3. Pop big rocks (20 points) into medium ones (50) and then small ones (100).
  4. Clear all the rocks to reach the next wave. Avoid bumps: you have 3 lives.
Controls
Aim and fireHold the mouse or your finger on the board, or Space to fire
Turn← → or A D
Thrust↑ or W, or hold far from the rocket
PauseP or Esc, or the pause button

Tips to score higher

  • Don’t fly too much. Staying near the middle and turning to shoot is often safer than zooming around.
  • Pop the small ones first. Small rocks are fast and worth the most. Clear them before splitting more big rocks.
  • Mind the edges. Rocks come back on the other side. A rock leaving the top will soon enter from the bottom.
  • Use the blink. After losing a life you’re safe while blinking. Use that time to get into a clear spot.
