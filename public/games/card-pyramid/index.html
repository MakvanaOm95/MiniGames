Card Pyramid

Card About 3 min

Click the cards. Or use Tab and Enter, and D to turn over a stock card. Press P to pause. Tap two cards that add up to 13. Tap a King on its own. Tap the red pile to turn over a card.

About Card Pyramid

Card Pyramid is our take on pyramid solitaire, a classic patience game. Twenty-eight cards are dealt face up in a pyramid. Your job is to take it apart by removing pairs of cards that add up to 13. Kings count as 13 all by themselves, so they can go alone.

You can only use cards that aren’t covered by other cards, plus the top card of the waste pile. When you run out of pairs, turn over a card from the stock. Clear the whole pyramid for a 50-point bonus and a brand-new pyramid, and keep going until you run out of moves.

How to play

  1. Press Play. A pyramid of 28 cards is dealt.
  2. Tap two uncovered cards that add up to 13 to remove them. Ace = 1, Jack = 11, Queen = 12.
  3. Tap a King to remove it on its own.
  4. Stuck? Tap the red stock pile to turn over a new card. When it’s empty you can flip the waste back over twice.
Controls
Pick a cardTap or click it (or Tab to it and press Enter)
Turn over a cardTap the stock pile, or press D
PauseP or Esc, or the pause button

Tips to score higher

  • Learn the pairs. A+Q, 2+J, 3+10, 4+9, 5+8, 6+7, and K alone. They soon become automatic.
  • Free the top. The peak card is the hardest to reach. Work toward it from the bottom row.
  • Don’t rush the stock. Check the whole pyramid for pairs before you turn over a new card.
  • Choose wisely. When two cards could pair with the same card, take the one that uncovers more cards.
