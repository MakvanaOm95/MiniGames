Block Drop

Puzzle About 5+ min

← → move, ↑ rotate, ↓ soft drop, Space hard drop, P pause. Use the buttons, or drag the board sideways, tap to rotate and flick down to drop.

About Block Drop

Block Drop is a falling-blocks puzzle. Shapes made of four squares fall one at a time into a tall well. Slide and rotate each one as it falls so it fits neatly with the blocks below. When a row is completely filled from wall to wall, it clears away and you score points.

Clear several rows at once for a big bonus: four rows in one go is worth the most. Every 10 rows the level goes up and the shapes fall a little faster. A dashed ghost shows exactly where the current shape will land, and the Next box in the score bar shows what’s coming.

How to play

  1. Press Play. A shape starts falling from the top.
  2. Move it left or right and rotate it to fit the gaps below.
  3. Fill a whole row to clear it. Clearing 2, 3 or 4 rows at once scores extra.
  4. The game ends when the stack reaches the top of the well.
Controls
Move← →, buttons, or drag sideways
Rotate↑, rotate button, or tap the board
Soft drop↓ or the down button (+1 point per row)
Hard dropSpace, drop button, or flick down (+2 per row)
PauseP or Esc

Tips to score higher

  • Keep the stack flat. A flat surface gives every shape somewhere to go. Bumpy stacks leave holes.
  • Leave one column open. Stack neatly and keep one side column empty, then drop a long bar in to clear four rows at once.
  • Use the Next box. Plan where the next shape will go while the current one is still falling.
  • Don’t cover holes. A gap buried under blocks is hard to clear. Fill gaps before building over them.
  • Watch the ghost. The dashed outline shows exactly where the shape will land, so you can hard-drop with confidence.
